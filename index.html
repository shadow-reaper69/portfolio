About

How I got here

I have liked taking technology apart and fixing what is broken since I was a child. Security is where that habit turned into something I want to do for a living.

What keeps me going is the moment a lab machine finally gives way after hours of failed attempts, and then working out and writing down why it worked.

Most of my time goes into PortSwigger Web Security Academy labs and TryHackMe rooms, using Burp Suite and Kali Linux, and into Python and Bash scripts that automate the repetitive parts of recon.

Projects

Defensive security · Wi-Fi monitoring

Cryo WiFi

A defensive detector that monitors captured Wi-Fi deauthentication-frame activity and alerts on unusually large bursts. In my observations, ordinary activity involved roughly 1–10 frames, compared with bursts of around 100–200 during deauthentication activity.

Reverse engineering · ESP32-C5 Super Mini

I reverse-engineered an open-source Wi-Fi security tool so it could work across more than one network on my ESP32-C5 Super Mini. Tested only on a network I own.
